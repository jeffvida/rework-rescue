Colour

Taken from the logo artwork. Deep teal and teal green carry the site, mint and gold are used sparingly, and warm ivory is the page background.

  • Deep teal #0F3A3APrimary. Headings, body text, dark sections.
  • Teal green #2E7D72Secondary. Buttons and links. White text on it passes AA.
  • Mint green #78C2A4Accent. Highlights and illustration. Not for small text on light backgrounds.
  • Warm gold #E6C078Highlight. Borders and fills. Use dark text on it.
  • Warm ivory #F8F7EDPage background.

Typography

The site uses each visitor's system fonts, with no font files loaded from anywhere. That keeps pages fast and keeps the privacy claim ("no third parties") true.
