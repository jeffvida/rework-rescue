Operations Reporting Review

A fictional example of the written review you could receive from me.

Fictional sample. This document illustrates the format and type of recommendations a customer may receive. It does not represent an actual client engagement or achieved result.

1. What I found

The business receives reports from multiple locations, but the information is not consistently structured.

Some of the issues identified include:

  • Different reporting formats across locations.
  • Inconsistent reporting periods.
  • Figures that do not reconcile.
  • Manual consolidation and checking.

2. Why it matters

These inconsistencies make it harder for management to compare locations and confidently use the consolidated report.

They also create repeated manual work every reporting period.

3. What I’d fix first

I would start by establishing consistent reporting definitions and introducing validation checks before information is consolidated.

This would help identify problems earlier rather than discovering them after the report has been prepared.

4. Recommended improvement

Create a standardised reporting process that:

  • Accepts consistent inputs from each location.
  • Checks missing and inconsistent information.
  • Reconciles regional figures.
  • Produces one consolidated management report.
  • Clearly identifies exceptions requiring attention.

5. Suggested next step

Review the existing reporting files and agree on a manageable first improvement.

The initial project should focus on one reporting process before considering wider changes.

Less time fixing the numbers. More time running the business.
