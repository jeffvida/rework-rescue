Privacy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This notice explains what information this website and Rework Rescue handle, why, and what you can ask for. Rework Rescue is run by Jeff Herson Vida, an independent specialist based in the Philippines. Jeff is responsible for your information, and you can reach him about privacy at jeffhersonvida@gmail.com.

In short

  • The free assessment runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not stored, and are only sent if you choose to share your result or book a review from it.
  • The chat assistant on the homepage sends what you type to my automation service and to Google (Gemini) to write a reply. Google may use these chats to improve its AI, so please leave out names, contact details and anything confidential.
  • This site uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts.
  • If you contact me or book a review, I use your details only to reply, hold the review and discuss possible work with you.
  • I don't sell your details, add you to a mailing list, or share them for marketing.

The assessment

  • Your answers are held in the page's memory while you use the assessment.
  • They are not put in the page address, the browser's history entries, local storage, session storage or cookies.
  • They are not sent anywhere while you complete the assessment.
  • If you refresh or leave the page, your answers are gone and you start again. Some browsers may restore a page from their back/forward cache, which can keep answers in memory for a short time.
  • "Print or save this page" uses your browser's own print function. This site does not keep or upload the printed copy.
  • The result comes from fixed rules in your browser. It does not call an AI service.
  • Under the result you can choose to share it with me. Book a free rework review lets you book a time; your result is sent with the booking, as described under “Booking a review” below. Email my result without booking opens a draft in your own email app with just your result. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and you can edit it first.

The chat assistant

  • The assistant on the homepage is an AI that helps you describe one report or process and suggests which service may fit. It can make mistakes; nothing it says is a quote or a commitment.
  • It only runs when you open it and send a message. Each message, together with the earlier messages in that chat, is sent to my automation service (n8n), which asks Google’s Gemini AI for a reply. The assistant uses Gemini’s free service, under which Google may use the messages and replies to improve its products, and people at Google may review them. Please don't include names, contact details or confidential information.
  • The conversation is kept in the page's memory only. Closing or refreshing the page ends it. The page remembers only that it has shown you the assistant's invitation, in your browser's session storage, until you close the tab.
  • For each reply, I keep a short log in my private Google Sheet: the time, a random chat number (so I can see which replies belong to the same chat; it isn't linked to you), which service was suggested, a one-sentence summary of what you described, how many messages the chat had, and whether the booking button was shown. The full messages are not logged, and runs that work are not saved in n8n. I delete log rows after 12 months.
  • To prevent abuse, the service counts messages per visitor per day using a one-way code made from your IP address and the date. The IP address itself is not stored, and the counts are cleared every day.
  • The assistant never books anything or sends anything on your behalf. Its booking button opens the same booking form described below.

The examples on the homepage

The two interactive examples ("From three messy exports to a reliable weekly report" and "Recurring bills across sites") use fictional data built into the page. Any decisions you click are kept only while the page is open. They run in your browser. You upload nothing, and nothing is sent, connected or stored.

When you contact me

What I receive. Your name, your email address or LinkedIn profile, and whatever you choose to write. If you share your assessment from the result page, I receive your name, email, company (optional), what you'd like to improve (optional), your assessment answers and result, the date and time, and, if the link you followed had them, its campaign tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign). The form shows exactly which assessment details are included before you send it.

Why I use it. To reply to you, arrange a rework review if you want one, and prepare a scope and price if you ask for one. I don't use it for anything else.

How it reaches me. At the moment you can reach me by email, which is handled by Google (Gmail), or by LinkedIn message, which is handled by LinkedIn. Each provider processes messages under its own privacy policy, and may store them on servers outside the Philippines. The inquiry form on this site is not connected yet and sends nothing. If a form service is added later, this notice will name it before the form goes live.

Booking a review. When you open “Book a free rework review”, the page asks my booking service which times are free. That request contains no details about you. When you book, the page sends your name, email, company (optional), the service you'd like help with (optional), the report or process you describe (optional), your time zone, the time you chose and, if you booked from the assessment, your result. The booking service checks my Google Calendar, creates the calendar event with a Google Meet link, sends you the invite, emails me the details and adds them to my private bookings spreadsheet, so I can keep track of reviews. It also emails you two reminders, one day and one hour before the review (the one-day reminder is skipped if you book less than a day ahead), and checks the calendar first so no reminder is sent for a review that has been cancelled or moved. To stop the same address booking several reviews at once, it keeps your email address and booking time until the review has passed.

Booking services. The booking service runs on n8n, which I host on Railway (Railway Corporation, United States). It holds your booking details only until the last reminder has been sent, and is set not to keep a record after that; if a booking fails, n8n may keep the details of that failed attempt for a short time so I can fix the problem. The calendar event, invite, video call and bookings spreadsheet are handled by Google (Google Calendar, Google Meet, Gmail and Google Sheets). Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy, and may store it on servers outside the Philippines. If online booking isn’t available, the pop-up offers an email draft instead, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

Please keep it general. Don't send confidential files or sensitive personal information about yourself, your staff or your customers. Describing the process in general terms is enough to start.

Who else sees it

Only me, plus the providers named above, which handle messages and bookings on my behalf. I share your details with anyone else only if you ask me to, or if the law requires it.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel (Vercel Inc., United States). Like any web host, Vercel processes technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. It keeps this in its logs for a limited time under its own privacy policy. I don’t use it to identify visitors.

How long I keep it

  • If your inquiry doesn't lead to a project, I delete your messages and details within 12 months of our last contact.
  • If we go ahead with a project, I keep project records for as long as the written agreement says and as long as the law requires for business and tax records.
  • You can ask me to delete your details sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012, you can ask to be informed about how your information is used, to access it, to correct it, to object to its use, to have it deleted or blocked, and to receive a copy in a common electronic format. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission (opens in a new tab). Depending on where you live, your own country's law may give you similar rights, and I'll honour those requests too.

To make a request, email jeffhersonvida@gmail.com. I'll reply within 30 days.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and loads no scripts, fonts or images from other websites. The only outside connection is to the booking service, and only after you open the booking pop-up. Links to LinkedIn and the National Privacy Commission open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this notice

If how I handle information changes, for example when a written inquiry form is added, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
